3D visualizations of symbols, sacred spaces and products

I create 3D visualizations and animations that show what can't be photographed: symbols in light and motion, church interiors from a new perspective, projects before they're built, or a parish history as a virtual book.

For Christian publishers and brands I visualize products - books, Bible editions, devotional items or albums - for launches, online stores and campaigns.

I work in Blender and After Effects, paying attention to light, materials and a mood that fits the sacred.

I work with churches, publishers and brands in Poland and abroad.

FAQ

What can you show in 3D?

Christian symbols, church interiors and details, stained glass, icons, projects before they're built and products for publishers and brands.

Do I need photos or plans?

They help - photos, floor plans or product samples. I build the 3D model from them.

Can the visualization be animated?

Yes. I can create still renders or an animation with camera movement, light and music.

Where can the visualizations be used?

Social media, websites, book launches, church screens and anniversary films.

How much does 3D visualization cost?

It depends on model complexity, number of shots and animation length. I quote individually.

How long does it take?

Usually two to several weeks, depending on scope.
