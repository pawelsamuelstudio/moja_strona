Bible stories and explainers for teaching, catechesis and outreach

I create animated Bible stories and explainers that tell in a few minutes what takes pages in text. Image, motion and narration help people understand the context, remember the message and come back to it.

I'm a long-time animator for Bible Unbound (USA), including “The Gospel in 5 Minutes” and stories of the apostles. I work in 2D, 3D and collage, matching the style to the audience and topic.

I also make explainers for foundations, publishers and ministries that want to show their mission, project or fundraiser simply.

I work with clients in Poland and abroad, including English-language projects.

Great for:

  • Bible stories on YouTube and social media
  • catechesis and Sunday school
  • retreats and small groups
  • Bible courses and platforms
  • presenting a ministry's mission
  • publisher materials

FAQ

Who are animated Bible stories for?

Bible platforms and channels, churches, ministries, teachers, publishers and foundations - anyone who wants to bring Scripture closer in an accessible way.

Can you help write the script?

Yes. I can work from your text or help shorten it and turn it into a script with narration and scenes.

What styles are possible?

2D, 3D, collage, watercolor illustration - I match the style to the audience (kids, youth, adults) and where it will be published.

Can the animation have voice-over in other languages?

Yes. I prepare English and Polish versions, and subtitles or voice-over can be added in more languages.

How much does it cost?

It depends on length, style and number of scenes. I quote each project individually after a short call.

How long does it take?

Usually a few weeks, depending on length and complexity. I agree the timeline before we start.
