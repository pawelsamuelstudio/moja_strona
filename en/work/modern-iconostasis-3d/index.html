Modern Iconostasis - 3D visualization

Client: Personal project / 3D concept of a sacred space

Project: Minimalist 3D visualization of an iconostasis

Role & Scope: Concept, 3D modeling, lighting and materials, camera animation, rendering

Challenge & Concept:
To show that the timeless form of the iconostasis - a symbolic gate between the visible and the invisible - can speak a contemporary language of simplicity, light and space without losing its spiritual depth.

Solution & Technique:
I built a restrained, balanced 3D model where proportion, light and silence matter most. The icons used in the project accompanied parish social media posts during the summer - here they return in three-dimensional space. It is an example of how a sacred space can be visualized for a church, community or publisher.
