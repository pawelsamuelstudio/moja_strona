Bible Unbound - “The Entire Life of Moses” intro

Client: Bible Unbound (USA)

Project: Animated intro for a film about the life of Moses

Role & Scope: Art Direction, illustration, 2D animation, watercolor transitions and textures, compositing

Challenge & Concept:
To draw viewers into the story within the first few seconds - warm, desert-like and symbolic - following the artistic direction requested by the Bible Unbound team.

Solution & Technique:
I designed a calm, organic look with soft transitions, subtle textures and motion resembling spreading watercolor. I paid special attention to colour and consistency with the channel's vision, so the intro feels like a natural doorway into the story of Moses.
