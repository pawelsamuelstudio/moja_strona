III Kierunek Uwielbienie Forum - “I Will Worship You in the Darkness”

Short-form videos for Reels and Shorts

Client: III Kierunek Uwielbienie Forum, Poland (2026)

Project: Promotional spots: horizontal version and short-form videos for Reels and Shorts

Role & Scope: Concept, motion design, animation, editing

Challenge & Concept:
The event theme - “I will worship You in the darkness” - speaks of faith not when everything works, but when it is empty, quiet and dark. Instead of a classic promo that explains and pushes a slogan, I wanted to capture exactly that tone.

Solution & Technique:
Darkness is not the background here, it is the content: a city at night, an empty road, a lone figure. Light appears sparingly, as a gentle point of reference. Shots are long and the rhythm slow - despite the short Reels and Shorts format, the spots don't rush, letting the viewer pause for a moment.
