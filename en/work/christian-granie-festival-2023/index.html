12th Christian Granie Festival - promo spots

Short vertical video

Client: Christian Granie Festival, Poland (12th edition, 2023)

Project: Festival promo series: main spot, debut artists teaser, short vertical video

Role & Scope: 2D animation, photo collage of performers, kinetic typography, editing to music

Challenge & Concept:
To invite people to a Christian music festival in an energetic, recognisable way - with spots matching the festival identity and working well both on YouTube and on social media.

Solution & Technique:
I built the spots on a dynamic collage: cut-out photos of performers combined with bold kinetic typography in the festival's green identity colour. The result is a main spot, a separate debut artists teaser and a short vertical video encouraging people to join.
