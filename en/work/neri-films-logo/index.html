NERI Films logo and logo animation

Other animated logos for Christian brands

Client: NERI Films - Catholic film studio

Project: Logo design, visual identity and logo animation

Role & Scope: Concept and symbolism, logo design, typography, logo animation

Challenge & Concept:
To capture the studio's spiritual roots and mission in a single mark. The name refers to St Philip Neri, patron of joy, and is also an acronym of the Latin motto Non Ego Requiescat Ignis - “I will not let the fire go out”.

Solution & Technique:
The central motif became a heart wrapped in flame - a symbol of love, devotion and creative passion that never fades. I combined classic elegance with contemporary minimalism and simple, strong typography. The logo animation ignites the flame around the heart and serves as the opening ident for the studio's films.
