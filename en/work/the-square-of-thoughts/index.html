The Square of Thoughts - visualization for Artur Ptak's exhibition

Client: Artur Ptak - exhibition “The Square of Thoughts - from allegory to an original sign”

Project: Animated visualization accompanying an art exhibition

Role & Scope: Concept, 2D animation, typography, editing to music (Michał Jacaszek, “Powoli”)

Challenge & Concept:
To extend the artist's series of works into a film that faithfully reflects the principles and Christian symbolism of his art, while showing a God who is eternal and acts outside of time, yet enters human history.

Solution & Technique:
The visualization takes up the themes of creation, incarnation and redemption - deliberately not in chronological order, which emphasises the many dimensions and coherence of God's action. Michał Jacaszek's meditative music creates space for contemplation. The film accompanied the exhibition as an integral part of the artistic experience.
