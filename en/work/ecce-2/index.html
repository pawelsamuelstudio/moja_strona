Ecce II

Project Type: Independent Animated Short / Experimental Animation

Scope of Work: Concept, Direction, Animation, Editing, Post-production

Author: Paweł Pauksztełło

Music: Paweł Łukowiec

Piano: Marek Mizera

Ecce II is the second part of the independent Ecce project, continuing its experimental and symbolic visual language.

The film is built around image, movement and editing, treating animation not simply as a technique but as a means of creating meaning. Individual forms, symbols and the relationships between them create a visual narrative that remains open to interpretation.

As in the first Ecce, the film relies on a restrained visual language and on combining abstract and recognizable elements. Movement, rhythm and composition guide the viewer through successive images rather than presenting a conventional linear story.

The visual composition is accompanied by music composed by Paweł Łukowiec, with piano performed by Marek Mizera. The music helps shape the rhythm and atmosphere of the film and remains an integral part of its experimental form.

Ecce II represents another stage in the search for a personal language of animation - combining experimental form, symbolism and visual storytelling.
