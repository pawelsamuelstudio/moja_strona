Colors

Project Type: Independent Animated Film / Experimental Animation

Scope of Work: Concept, Direction, Design, Animation, Editing, Post-production

Author: Paweł Pauksztełło

Year: 2014

Colors is an independent animated film presenting an artistic and creative interpretation of the city of Kielce. The project uses color, form, movement and rhythm to create a personal image of the city and the emotions connected with its urban space.

Color is not used merely as an aesthetic element. It becomes one of the main storytelling tools, creating associations, emotions and relationships between individual elements of the animated world.

The film uses abstract form and visual metaphor to transform the experience of the city into a short, dynamic animated composition.

“Colors” won 1st Place in the first edition of the competition organized by the Kino Mówi Film Culture Association in Kielce in 2014.

It was the first award-winning animated film in my portfolio and one of the projects that helped define the direction of my later personal work in animation, motion design and visual storytelling.
