XIV Worship “In the Light of Christ” - promo series

Vertical 9:16 spots - guests and performers

Client: XIV Worship in the City Centre, Kielce, Poland (2026)

Project: Social media video campaign: 5 vertical 9:16 spots + one 16:9 summary spot

Role & Scope: Series concept, 3D animation, graphic design, format versions for social media and YouTube

Challenge & Concept:
Building interest in the event through mobile-first communication - with each post introducing one invited guest or performer, while the whole series stays instantly recognisable.

Solution & Technique:
I created five short vertical animations, each presenting one guest, plus a horizontal version bringing all participants together in a single composition for YouTube and presentations. One consistent visual style ties all the pieces together, so the series works as a coherent campaign.
