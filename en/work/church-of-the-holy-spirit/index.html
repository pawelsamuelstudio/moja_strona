Church of the Holy Spirit - 3D Animated Parish History

Client: Holy Spirit Parish

Project: Animated Anniversary Film / Promotional Spot

Role & Scope: Art Direction, 3D Modeling, 3D / 2.5D Animation, Compositing, Post-production

Challenge & Concept:
Presenting 35 years of rich parish history in a cohesive, dynamic, and visually engaging manner. The main goal was to transform archival photography into a modern, cinematic story showcasing the community, the construction of successive temples, and milestone moments.

Solution & Technique:
I constructed a 3D virtual model of an interactive book. Using advanced 3D compositing and spatial animation techniques, I blended static archival photos with volumetric lighting effects and fluid camera movements. The turning pages establish a seamless narrative flow, giving historical photographs depth, dimension, and new life.
