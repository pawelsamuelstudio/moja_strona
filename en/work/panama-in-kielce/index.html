Panama in Kielce - Animated Promotional Film

Client / Event: World Youth Day

Project: Animated Event Promo Spot

Role & Scope: Concept, Motion Design, 2D Animation, Post-production

Challenge & Goal:
Creating a dynamic promo animation for those unable to attend the central World Youth Day celebrations in Panama, but participating in the satellite event held at Targi Kielce. The primary goal was to bring the energy and vibrant spirit of this international gathering to the local audience.

Concept & Execution:
The visual concept was built around the event's official logo. Using bright, energetic motion graphics, I created a visual narrative designed to evoke a sense of joy, unity, and excitement, effectively bridging the distance and bringing the atmosphere of Panama closer to the attendees in Kielce.
