REDEMPTIO – Experimental Christian Animation

Project: Experimental Personal Animation / Motion Design

Role and Scope: Concept, Visual Design, Motion Design, Animation, Editing, Post-production

Idea and Concept:
REDEMPTIO is an experimental visual story exploring the inner struggle with sin, the experience of forgiveness and the moment of opening oneself to God. Rather than relying on literal storytelling, the project uses imagery, motion, rhythm and symbolism to communicate a spiritual transformation in an emotional way while leaving room for personal interpretation.

Approach and Visual Form:
The animation is structured as a dynamic visual sequence in which images and transitions gradually lead the viewer from tension and inner conflict towards forgiveness, hope and an encounter with God. The experimental motion design approach makes it possible to communicate spiritual ideas through atmosphere, symbolism and emotion without relying on conventional narrative storytelling.
