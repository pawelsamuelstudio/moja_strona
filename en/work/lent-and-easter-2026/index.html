From Silence to Light - Lent and Easter 2026

Client: Holy Spirit Parish, Kielce, Poland

Project: Series of looping 3D animations for Lent + Easter visualization

Role & Scope: Series concept, 3D modeling and animation, lighting, editing of the final compilation

Challenge & Concept:
To accompany the parish community through Lent online as well - in a restrained, quiet and focused form that is not mere decoration, but helps people live through each stage of the journey towards Easter.

Solution & Technique:
I created a series of short 3D loops based on elements well known to parishioners: fragments of architecture, light, shadow and details of the church interior. Successive animations accompanied Ash Wednesday, the Stations of the Cross, Palm Sunday and the Triduum. For Easter I gathered them into one visualization - from silence and waiting to the light of the Resurrection.
