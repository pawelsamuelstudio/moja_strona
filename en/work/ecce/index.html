Ecce - Animated Short Film

Project Type: Independent Animated Short / Experimental Animation

Scope of Work: Concept, Direction, Photography, Animation, Editing, Post-production

Author: Paweł Pauksztełło

Ecce is an independent animated short exploring the mystery of human redemption. The film reflects on humanity's inability to overcome the fractures and consequences of sin through its own strength, and on the need for an intervention that comes from beyond the self - the mercy of the Redeemer.

The film combines computer animation with fragments of original photography. Its imagery creates a restrained, symbolic narrative in which meaning is built not only through movement, but also through the relationship between forms, light and visual signs.

Mathematical symbols appearing between individual sequences act as both commentary and interpretative keys. Resembling equations, they guide the viewer through a visual reflection on the human condition, purification and redemption.

Ecce was created in 2018 as an independent personal film project and was presented at animation and experimental film festivals.
