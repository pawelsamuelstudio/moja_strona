If Or While

Project Type: Original Video Composition / Experimental Animation / Motion Design

Scope of Work: Concept, Design, Animation, Editing, Post-production

Author: Paweł Pauksztełło

If Or While is an original experimental video composition accompanied by piano music. The project is a meditative journey through abstract geometric structures, symbolism, organic forms and sacred motifs.

The imagery develops in close relationship with the music, moving between ordered geometric forms and more organic structures. Movement, composition, light and the changing relationships between visual elements create a calm, contemplative visual narrative.

Symbolism plays an important role throughout the project. Abstract signs, forms and sacred motifs do not create a single literal story, but instead leave space for personal interpretation and reflection.

The title If Or While refers to concepts borrowed from programming languages. Within the project, however, they are separated from their purely technical meaning and become part of the visual language of the composition.

The project is part of my personal experimental work, combining animation, abstraction, music, symbolism and visual storytelling while exploring a form somewhere between motion design, experimental film and meditative visual art.
