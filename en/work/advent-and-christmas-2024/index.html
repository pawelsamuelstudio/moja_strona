Advent and Christmas 2024 - animated illustrations

Individual animated illustrations from the series

Client: Parish social media communication

Project: Series of looping animated illustrations for Advent, Christmas and Epiphany

Role & Scope: Illustration, 2D animation, loops prepared for social media

Challenge & Concept:
To accompany the community throughout Advent and the Christmas season with regular posts that stay visually consistent, while each one tells about a different sign or event of this time.

Solution & Technique:
I created a series of short animated illustrations in one style: from a Rorate Mass poster and Advent candles, through images from Advent readings (the shoot from the stump of Jesse, the mountain of the Lord, the messianic banquet, Zechariah and John the Baptist), to the Star of Bethlehem, the stable and the C+M+B inscription on a door. Each loop works as a standalone post, and together they form one story.
