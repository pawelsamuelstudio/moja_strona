Stage visuals for the 40th Diocesan World Youth Day

Client: 40th World Youth Day of the Diocese of Kielce (Kielce Congress Centre, 22 Nov 2025)

Project: Looping animated backgrounds and intro for stage / LED screens

Role & Scope: 2D/3D animation, motion elements design, loops prepared for live stage playback

Challenge & Concept:
Creating visuals consistent with the official event identity that would also work together with stage lighting, music and the energy of a day-long youth gathering.

Solution & Technique:
The starting point was the official poster designed by Jakub Radek. Based on it, I built motion elements, looping animated backgrounds and an opening intro. On site, the graphics worked together with light and music, becoming part of the living stage space rather than just a picture in the background.
