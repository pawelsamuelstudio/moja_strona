FAQ

Do you design logos from scratch or only animate them?

Both. I can design a new mark or bring your existing logo to life.

Who is this service for?

Ministries, churches, events, foundations, publishers and brands with a Christian worldview.

Do I need a vector logo?

Ideally yes, but if you don't have one, get in touch - we'll see what we can work with, or I can redraw it.

What formats will I receive?

For videos, social media, livestreams and presentations - including transparent backgrounds.

How much does it cost?

It depends on whether we design the mark from scratch and on the animation style. I quote individually.

How long does it take?

Animating an existing logo usually takes a few days; a new mark with animation takes a few weeks.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes, including English-language projects.
