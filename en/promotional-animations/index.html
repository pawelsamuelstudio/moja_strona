Promos that invite people and build anticipation

I create animated promos for retreats, festivals, worship nights, conferences and community gatherings. Instead of a static poster - a short story that sets the mood first and gives the details at the end.

I can prepare a full campaign: a main spot, a series of short vertical videos introducing guests and performers, and a version for screens at the event itself.

I've worked for Worship in the City Centre (Kielce), the Kierunek Uwielbienie Forum and the Christian Granie Festival, among others.

FAQ

What events can be promoted with animation?

Retreats, festivals, worship concerts, conferences, youth gatherings, parish feasts, anniversaries, fundraisers - any event you want people to come to.

How far in advance should I get in touch?

Ideally 4-6 weeks before you plan to publish. For tighter deadlines, write anyway and I'll see what's possible.

Will you prepare versions for different platforms?

Yes. A horizontal spot for YouTube and screens, vertical Reels for social media and short guest announcements.

Can you use our poster and branding?

Yes. I often start from the event poster or logo and bring it to life in motion.

How much does a promo cost?

It depends on length, number of versions and style. I quote each project individually.

Can you also create visuals for the event itself?

Yes - LED screen backgrounds, stage intros and transitions. See also worship backgrounds.
