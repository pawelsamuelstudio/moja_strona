Hello and welcome

My name is Paweł Pauksztełło. I'm an independent motion designer and 2D & 3D animator from Kielce, Poland, specializing in visual communication for Christian ministries, organizations and media creators.


I combine my experience in animation and visual storytelling with a Master's degree in Theology and experience teaching religious education. This allows me to translate complex biblical, theological and educational concepts into engaging, accessible visual stories.


I collaborate internationally with Christian content creators, animation studios and organizations, including Bible Unbound (USA), producing animated explainers, educational content, promotional videos and motion graphics. I am available for both individual projects and long-term creative partnerships. Portfolio


Outside of work, I spend time with my wife and three sons. I am also involved in my parish as a lector and help organize worship events in my city.
