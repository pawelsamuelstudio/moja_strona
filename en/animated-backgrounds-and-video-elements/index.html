Backgrounds and visuals for worship, services and events

I create looping animated backgrounds for LED screens and projectors, opening intros and transitions between parts of the event. I design them to work with lighting, music and event branding without pulling attention away from prayer.

I also create sermon and retreat series graphics: title slides, lyric backgrounds and short livestream openers.

One example is the stage visuals for the 40th World Youth Day of the Diocese of Kielce, built from the official poster.

I deliver files in the formats and aspect ratios your screens need.

FAQ

What screens do you create backgrounds for?

LED walls, projectors and TVs - in whatever aspect ratio and resolution you need, including unusual wide stage screens.

Are the backgrounds looped?

Yes. I make seamless loops that can play for as long as you need without a visible jump.

Can you match the visuals to our event poster?

Yes. I often build the visuals from the official poster and branding so everything feels consistent.

Can you make lyric backgrounds?

Yes - calm, readable backgrounds where lyrics display clearly.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of backgrounds, length and style. I quote individually after a short call.

Can we license ready-made animations for Lent or Easter?

Get in touch - some of my original animations, such as Redemptio and Ecce, can be licensed to churches.
